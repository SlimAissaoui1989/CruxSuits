# CruxSuits code of conduct

Everyone who contributes to CruxSuits should be able to participate without harassment or discrimination. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other contributors with respect, regardless of their background or experience.
- Keep feedback specific and constructive. Discuss the work and give people a chance to respond.
- Respect other people's privacy and share personal information only with their consent.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, and sharing someone's private information without consent are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner using the contact information on their GitHub profile, if available. You can also use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive content. Include links and enough context to explain what happened. Keep personal details and sensitive material out of public issues.

Maintainers will review reports and limit disclosure to what is needed to investigate and respond. Depending on the severity and whether the behavior recurs, maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, or restrict participation. Report suspected software vulnerabilities through the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
